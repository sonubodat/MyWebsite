This page covers **this website only**. Each app has its own policy, listed below.

## What this website collects
- It sets no cookies, has no forms, no user accounts, no ads and no analytics.
- The hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the service, under its own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
- If you email me, I receive your address and what you write. I use it to answer you and keep it as long as needed for that and for legal records.

## Your rights
You can ask me what I hold about you, and ask me to correct or delete it. Email the address at the bottom of this page. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.
