Email the address below with your device, app version, player tag, what happened and when.

## Lost progress
The guest account lives on your device. Reinstalling or clearing app data can lose it.

## Reward not received
Tell us the date and time you watched the ad.

## Match problems
Tell us about lag, disconnects or a wrong result, and roughly when it happened.

## Cheating or bad names
Report the player in the app, or email us with the player name.

## Your data
Change choices in Settings, Privacy choices. See [delete your account](/taptap/delete-account) and the [privacy policy](/taptap/privacy-policy).
