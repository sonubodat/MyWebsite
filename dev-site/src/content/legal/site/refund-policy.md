## Purchases made through Apple or Google
In-app purchases and subscriptions are billed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Their refund rules apply and only they can send the money back.

- Apple: request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).
- Google Play: see [Google Play refunds](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Cancelling a subscription
Cancel in your store account settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Access continues until the paid period ends. Deleting the app or your account does **not** cancel a subscription.
