Email the address below with your device, app version and what happened. Do not include journal text you want to keep private.

**In danger or crisis?** Do not wait for email. Call 112, or Tele-MANAS on 14416.

## Do I need an account?
No. Signing in with Google or Apple is optional and lets your journal and subscription follow you to a new device.

## Subscription
Cancel in your store account settings. Cancelling does not delete anything you wrote. See [refunds](/refund-policy).

## Microphone
To talk to Omi, allow microphone access in your device settings.

## Your data
