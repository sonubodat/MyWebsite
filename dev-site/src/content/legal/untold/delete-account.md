---
title: Delete your account
updated: 27 November 2026
---

## In the app
Open **Settings**, choose **Delete account** and confirm.

## Without the app
Email the address below from the address you signed in with, with the subject "UNTOLD account deletion". If you used Apple's private email, tell us your nickname and the approximate date you joined.

## What is deleted
Your profile, journal, moods, notes, Omi conversations, Wall posts, peer chat records, psychologist records and your sign-in account. This cannot be undone. Backup copies expire on each provider's schedule. Records the law requires us or a payment provider to keep, such as purchase records held by the stores, are not removed by this.

**Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription.** Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

## Delete only some data
You can delete individual Wall posts and clear the Omi conversation without closing your account.
