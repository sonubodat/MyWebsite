Privacy Policy — UNTOLD

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the UNTOLD app (“UNTOLD”, “the app”) collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what choices you have. UNTOLD is an anonymous emotional support app for iOS and Android with a journal and mood tracker, Omi (an AI listener), a shared Wall, peer chat, meditation content and, where available, sessions with psychologists.

The publisher named at the bottom of this page (“we”, “us”) is responsible for your information and is the data controller (the “Data Fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023).

What you share here can be sensitive, including information about your mental health. Please read this policy before you use the app.

Anonymous means anonymous to other users. Other users see a nickname and an avatar, not your name or email. It does not mean we hold nothing about you.

The short version

  • You can use the journal without signing in. Without sign-in, your journal stays on your device.
  • If you sign in with Google or Apple, your account and content are stored on our servers so they can sync.
  • What you type to Omi is sent to a third-party AI model provider so that Omi can reply.
  • Peer chat messages are end-to-end encrypted and we do not store their content.
  • We use Meta’s SDK to measure our advertising. We never send journal, mood or conversation content to Meta.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money.
  • You can delete your account and data in the app.
  • UNTOLD is not therapy and not an emergency service.

Words used in this policy

  • Personal data: information about an identified or identifiable person, including a random ID.
  • Sensitive data: data that can reveal health, including mental health, such as journal entries, moods and conversations.
  • Content: what you write, say, upload or post.
  • Processing: anything done with data, such as collecting, storing, using, sharing or deleting it.
  • Provider: a company that processes data to help run the app.

Where the information comes from

  • From you: profile answers, journal entries, moods, notes, conversations, voice, posts, reports, uploads and emails.
  • From Google or Apple when you sign in: an account identifier, and the email address and name they pass to us. Apple lets you hide your email.
  • Automatically: device and app details, time zone, first and last use, and advertising measurement events.
  • From Apple, Google and RevenueCat: whether a subscription or trial is active.
  • From other users: reports and blocks that involve you.

Psychologists

If you apply as a psychologist we also collect your full name, email, phone, qualifications, licence details and documents, profile photo and bio, availability, and bank or UPI details for payouts. We use them to check your application, publish your profile, run bookings and pay you. Your public profile details are visible to users. If you book a session we record both accounts, the time and the price.

Sensitive data and your consent

Journal entries, moods, notes and Omi conversations can reveal your mental health. We process them only to provide the features you choose to use. By writing or speaking in these features you ask us to process that data for you. You can stop at any time, delete what you wrote, or delete your account.

We do not use journal, mood, meditation or conversation content for advertising, and we never send it to Meta.

Omi, our AI listener

  • Omi is an AI, not a person. It can be wrong, incomplete or unsuitable.
  • When you use Omi, your message, and the parts of the conversation needed to reply, are sent to a third-party AI model provider that generates the answer on our behalf.
  • When you speak to Omi, your voice recording is turned into text, and Omi’s reply into speech, by a speech service. We do not store the recording.
  • Do not tell Omi names, addresses, account numbers or other details that identify you or someone else.
  • You can clear the Omi conversation from its screen.
  • If you do not want your words processed by an AI provider, do not use Omi. The journal works without it.

The Wall

Wall posts and reactions are visible to other users of UNTOLD without your name. Anyone who sees a post can read, remember or copy it, so do not include details that identify you or others. You can delete your own posts. To report a post, email us with its text and the time you saw it.

Peer chat

Peer chat connects two adults about a topic. Messages are end-to-end encrypted: we cannot read them and we do not store them. The other person can still read, copy or photograph what you send. We keep a record of the topic, who was matched, times and blocks. If you report someone, we receive the reason and anything you choose to include.

Microphone, photos, files and other permissions

  • Microphone: only when you talk to Omi.
  • Photo library, camera and files: only when you choose to upload something, such as a psychologist profile photo or licence document.
  • Notifications and background audio: to show playback controls and keep meditation audio playing.
  • Internet: to sync, talk to Omi, post and play content.
  • Tracking (iPhone): iOS asks whether your advertising identifier may be used to measure our ads. Saying no does not limit any feature.

Advertising measurement

UNTOLD shows no third-party ads in the app. We advertise UNTOLD and measure the results with the Meta SDK. It can record that the app was installed or opened, that you registered, started a trial or bought a subscription, with plan and price, together with technical device information. On iPhone the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking; events are still sent without it. Meta processes this data under its privacy policy. You can control ads in your Meta account and device settings, and you can object by emailing us.

We require providers to use data to provide their service to us. We do not sell your personal data for money. Measurement through Meta may be treated as “sharing” or “targeted advertising” under some US state laws.

When someone may be in danger

UNTOLD does not monitor your journal or peer chat for emergencies and cannot promise to notice a crisis. If information that reaches us suggests a serious and immediate risk to life, or abuse of a child, we may share what is necessary with emergency services or authorities where the law allows or requires it.

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the content or your account, changing device permissions or emailing us.

Age

People under 18 can use UNTOLD only with the permission of a parent or guardian. Peer chat is only for people who state they are 18 or older. UNTOLD is not directed at children under 13, or under a higher minimum age where your country sets one. A parent or guardian who wants a child’s data seen or removed can email us.

How long we keep it

Journal entries, moods, notes and Omi conversations are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Wall posts are kept until you delete them, we remove them or your account is deleted. Account and profile data are kept while the account exists. Records needed for safety, payments, tax or a legal claim may be kept as long as the law requires. Backup copies expire on each provider’s schedule.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete individual Wall posts, clear Omi, or delete your whole account. See delete your UNTOLD account. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Data held by Apple, Google or Meta under their own terms is managed by them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what we hold and get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to processing, receive it in a portable format, withdraw consent, opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising, appeal a refusal, and not be treated worse for using a right.

Email the address at the bottom of this page, from the address you signed in with where possible. We may ask for information that shows the account is yours. We answer within the time the law requires.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland

You have the GDPR or UK GDPR rights above, including rights over health data, and may complain to your local supervisory authority, or to the Information Commissioner’s Office in the UK.

United States

Residents of California and other states with privacy or consumer health data laws may ask to know, access, correct and delete data, withdraw consent for health data and opt out of sale or sharing. The categories we collect are identifiers, account details, health-related information you enter, audio you send, content, subscription records, device and network information and advertising measurement events.

India

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct and erase your personal data, withdraw consent, nominate a person to act for you and raise a grievance. See the grievance officer. If it is not resolved, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Other places

Email us and we will handle your request under the law that applies to you.

International transfers

We are based in India. Some providers process data outside India and outside your country, including in the United States. Where the law requires a transfer safeguard, such as standard contractual clauses, we rely on the safeguards offered by those providers.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, peer chat messages are end-to-end encrypted and database access rules limit users to their own records. No system is completely secure. Keep your phone locked and your Google or Apple account protected. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities where the law requires it.

Do Not Track

UNTOLD is an app and does not respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Use the device controls described above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, our providers or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest version. For material changes we will give notice in the app or by another reasonable way, and ask for consent where the law requires it.

Not a crisis service

If you may be in danger, contact emergency services now (112 in India) or Tele-MANAS (14416). See the disclaimer.

Contact

For questions, requests or complaints about privacy, email the address at the bottom of this page and mention “UNTOLD”. See also support, terms, community guidelines and the grievance officer.
