title: Community Guidelines
updated: 27 November 2026
---

These apply to the Wall, peer chat and any other place where you reach another person.

## Do not
- bully, harass or threaten anyone
- post hate speech
- encourage self-harm or suicide, or describe methods
- post sexual content, and never any sexual content involving a minor
- share names, contacts or other identifying details, yours or anyone's
- ask to move a conversation off the app, ask for money, sell or spam
- pretend to be a professional

## Report and block
In peer chat, use **report** or **block**. To report a Wall post, email the address below with the post's text and the time you saw it. You can delete your own posts.

## What we do
We review reports and may remove content or restrict or close accounts. Serious risk to life or abuse of a child may be reported to the authorities.

## If someone is at risk
Tell them to contact emergency services (112 in India) or Tele-MANAS (14416).
