This page is provided under India's Information Technology Rules, 2021 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The grievance officer for this website and my apps is the person named at the bottom of this page. Write to that email address with the subject "Grievance".

## Include
- your name and a way to reply
- the app name and your username or player tag
- what your complaint is about, with dates or screenshots

## Timelines
- Acknowledgement within 24 hours.
- Resolution within 15 days of receipt.
