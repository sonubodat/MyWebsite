## In the app
1. Open TapTap and go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Legal**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, read the list and confirm.

## On the web
Use the [TapTap deletion form](https://tapntap.dpdns.org/delete-account.html) with your player tag or username, plus your deletion code if you have it. Or email the address below with your player tag. We may ask for more details to confirm the account is yours.

## What happens

| Data | Result |
|---|---|
| Account ID, player tag, display name, username, avatar | Removed within 30 days |
| Friends, requests, blocks, social settings, consents | Removed within 30 days |
| Analytics profile for your player ID | Deleted within 30 days |
| Match results and coin records | Kept, anonymised, so opponents' history stays correct |
| Reports about your account | Kept anonymised for 90 days, then deleted |

Your sessions end at once. Backup copies expire on each provider's schedule. Accounts inactive for 24 months are anonymised automatically.
